Leonid Ivanov

Autonomous systems researcher · Co-founder & CEO of Adanomy

Autonomous systems · Physical AI · Product R&D

I develop autonomous transport systems and turn research prototypes into software that can be integrated, tested and operated on real vehicles.

My work spans motion planning and neural mapping, Autoware-based vehicle integration, data infrastructure and fleet management. At Adanomy, I combine CEO responsibilities with technical leadership and ownership of product strategy, customer discovery and early sales.

adanomy Current venture

Autonomy software for freight transport

Adanomy is developing a portable autonomous-driving stack, a data platform and a fleet management system for commercial freight operations. We are currently validating priority use cases and customer segments with logistics and industrial partners.

As co-founder and CEO, I lead product and business strategy, system architecture and R&D, while also running customer discovery, early sales, partnerships and preparation of the first pilots.
